Digital Menus For Restaurants

You can display your menu on a wall mounted smart TV, on a personal computer, tablet, pad, or “smart phone”, from your own network, website or server. You can also share your menu with customers by sending them a link where they can browse your menu or by scanning a qr-code. There are several common choices about where & how to show your digital menus to current or prospective clients. Ideally, a restaurant's menu should display magnificently, clearly & be legible on all of the devices mentioned above; regardless of their size.

All of the above-mentioned devices can access menus on proprietary applications, software, servers or other platforms. However, the most viable, expedient, user-friendly & broadly viewed is on a domain name, on the internet. Yes, on your own website designed specifically for you. With your restaurant's logo, illustrations, slogans, introduction, photographs & menus. Some venues have several menus (all different), others just one. For example; executive menu, lunch menu, dinner menu, a la carte, delivery menu, take-out menu & others.

For mobile or “smart phones” your domain can be accessed by typing out a domain name in the address bar os a search engine, clicking a link or by scanning a QR-code with the phone's camera or QR-code reader.


Why Smart Telephone, PC, Or Television Menus?

  • Imagine never having to clean or print another menu!

  • Save money, never reprint due to a typographical error or menu change!

  • 100% sanitary, there aren't shared surfaces. It'll bring peace of mind to your staff & guests! With the outbreak of Coronavirus, digital menus became a much safer alternative to paper menus!

  • Faster service & less frustrating for your customers. Having a wall mounted smart television or a qr code on each table avoids delays in showing guests their menus.

  • A digital menu is always clean, new, crisp & sparkling. Restaurants don't need to cleanse, wipe or replace them repeatedly.

  • Digital menus are easier to read, attractive and well organized.

  • Each restaurant may have multiple menus, or just one (e.g. à la carte, daily menu, excutive menu, dinner menu, lunch menu, take-ut menu, delivery menu, etc.).

  • Online the menu is always accessible, it can be read from any location, at any time.

  • Open a menu instantly, without stress; no application or software to install. Read the menu without having to register, advertising or pop-ups.

  • Easy access, with QR code or link - You can easily open a menu using a QR code placed at the entrance or at the tables in the restaurant.


QR Codes & the Internet

QR codes can act as a connection between people & the Internet. As smartphones have evolved, it's become easier for people to access websites or resources by scanning a QR code. People usually don't even need to download a special QR code reader. They just open their camera & the phone will do the rest. For androids or smart phones that don't have the capability, google play has many free apps.

A QR code which is abbreviated from Quick Response Code. It is a two dimensional barcode. They were invented by a Japanese automotive firm, Denso Wave. It's essentially an optical label that has information about the item to which it's directed. They're used for many different applications or tasks.

Qr codes are made up of black squares arranged on a square grid, placed on a light background. This can be read by an imaging device such a a “smart phone” camera. On the Internet they are widely used to be scanned by a cell phone & pointed to a URL (Uniform Resource Locator) or website address.

I've used them for business cards, restaurant menus, pamphlets & many other creative uses. For restaurants it saves money because menus never have to be printed. Each table has a folded card that has a printed QR code, the customers scan the code & see the menus on their cell phone or pad.
