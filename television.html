Large Screen TVs For Displaying Digital Menus Or Special Offers, In-House

Restaurants are frequented because of the quality and flavor of their food. The menu is one of the principle factors that attract clients. It's usually the first point of contact, it creates the first impressions. Traditionally, the majority of restaurants use printed paper menus. The owners spend a great deal of time & money to be able to offer appetizing, attractive menus. After a month or two this beautiful menu is unrecognizable. Even if they are laminated, they look dirty & worn.


A Viable Option To Replace Printed Menus

More and more restaurants are using digital menus, instead of printed ones. For many years restaurants have been using specialized signage devices or digital board displays to show customers their menus digitally, in-house. Undoubtedly, there are some that are very sophisticated & expensive. Without going in to detail, they usually cost many times more than a simple smart tv. Today's smart televisions offer impressive resolutions & functions for a fraction of the price.

A digital menu displayed on a smart TV accentuates it, projecting it on a screen in an appealing, organized way. It's less frustrating, easier to read & order from. Today's smart televisions are very ergonomic, user-friendly, inexpensive & have impressive resolutions! The days of straining to read messy paper or dirty smudges is over! Digital menus always look, clean & crisp.


How Are “Digital Signage” Or “Digital Menu Boards” & Smart Televisions Different?

Though there are several differences, the main one is that a television is a self contained, stand-alone working system. They have functioning sound systems, tuners etc. They have many capacities which permit them to work as a “stand-alone” device. Signage displays are similar to large computer monitors, requiring support from other devices or applications to work.
