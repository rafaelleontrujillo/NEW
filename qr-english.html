QR Codes & the Internet

QR codes can act as a connection between people & the Internet. As smartphones have evolved, it's become easier for people to access websites or resources by scanning a QR code. People usually don't even need to download a special QR code reader. They just open their camera & the phone will do the rest. For androids or smart phones that don't have the capability, google play has many free apps.

A QR code which is abbreviated from Quick Response Code. It is a two dimensional barcode. They were invented by a Japanese automotive firm, Denso Wave. It's essentially an optical label that has information about the item to which it's directed. They're used for many different applications or tasks.

Qr codes are comprised of black squares arranged on a square grid, placed on a light background. This can be read by an imaging apparatus such a a cel phone camera. It's processed using Reed-Salomon error correction & then deciphered.On the Internet for example, they are widely used to be scanned by a cellular phone & pointed to a URL (Uniform Resource Locator) or website address.

I've used them for business cards, restaurant menus, pamphlets & many other creative uses. On a business card it's easier for someone to scan the code that to have to manually type a domain for your website.For restaurants it saves money because menus never have to be printed. Each table has a folded card that has a printed QR code, the customers scan the code & see the menus on their cel phone or pad.
